# 加熱 is a standalone list of notes

- **Standalone, as TBD is.** What a heating note is about is usually a bought
  thing - 冷凍吐司, 香腸, a boxed meal - with no place in the ingredient or dish
  library, so the note names it in words and points at nothing. An
  ingredient's own heating guide (`ingredient_heating`) stays on the
  ingredient: structured, about one raw ingredient, read from its page.
- **A name and free text, nothing more.** The owner asked for "just simple
  note of how to heat/reheat food". Method, temperature and time as fields
  would cover the oven and miss the 「蓋子打開一角」 that is the point of half
  these notes.
- **The name is required.** A TBD entry may be nameless because its links say
  what it is; a heating note without a name is text nobody can find.
- **TBD's shape, copied rather than shared.** Two instances is the second
  repetition, not the third; a generic "ordered standalone list" component
  waits for a third page that wants it.
