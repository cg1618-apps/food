# A new recipe starts blank or as a copy, and there are no templates

**Recipe templates (範本)** - named skeletons a new recipe could start from,
with their own table, routes, 設定 tab and 存成範本 - were removed at the
owner's request. Copying an existing recipe covers the same need.

**The chooser stays, with two choices.** 空白 and 複製另一份食譜 are different
starts, and the copy needs a search for the recipe to copy, so the question is
worth asking before the form.

**A copy takes the structure and the notes, not what makes it that recipe.**
Name, sources, status and pictures stay with the source; the copy starts on
the first status like any new recipe. Its dish comes along - a copy is most
often another way of making the same dish - unless `?dish=` names one.

**The choice is in the URL** (`?blank=1`, `?from=`), so the form survives a
reload and Back returns to the question.
