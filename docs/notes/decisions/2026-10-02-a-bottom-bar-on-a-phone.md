# A navigation bar fixed to the bottom of the screen on a phone

A divergence from `media`, whose navigation folds below `lg` into a menu
button that opens a full-screen drawer. media has a catalogue of sections and
sub-pages to fold away. food has a handful of destinations that fit in one row
under a thumb, and the app is opened one-handed in a shop or at the stove,
where a menu button and a drawer are two taps and a covered screen for every
move. Pages are padded at the bottom so the bar never covers content.
