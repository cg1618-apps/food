# A caption belongs to the attachment, not to the picture

The caption sits beside `focus` on the gallery row, for the same reason: one
picture in a dish's gallery and in a recipe's may need to say different
things, and a caption on `image` would make editing one silently edit the
other.

It has no length limit, because no free-text field here has one: a caption is
short by nature, and a cap would be the first and only one in the app.
